Other major announcements:

Sana Coach: Helps prepare performance reviews, synthesize feedback, and interpret employee sentiment. Early access, with additional coaching capabilities coming later.  
Work Intelligence: Breaks jobs into tasks and required skills, helping leaders identify opportunities for AI and employee development.  
Expanded Decision Intelligence: Lets HR and finance ask questions, compare workforce and financial scenarios, and act using governed Workday data.  
Workday Candidate Profile: A reusable, candidate-controlled profile that prefills applications across Workday career sites. Expected early 2027.  
Data Cloud and Trusted Agent Platform: Governed access to live Workday data, plus Agent Harness, Developer Agent, Custom Agents, and MCP tools for building and running agents. These core capabilities are generally available, with the development tools included in Workday Extend AI.  
Open Developer Program and Agent Passport: Public API/MCP documentation, a beta for requesting personal development tenants, and early-access agent verification records.  
Sana in other tools: Claude Cowork integration is early access. Existing self-service is available in Gemini Enterprise, Copilot, Teams, and Slack; broader Sana capabilities are planned for late 2026.  
C1.ai acquisition agreement: Intended to expand identity and access governance for employees and agents across connected systems. An announced acquisition agreement, rather than a released feature.
