FlatRatePA LLC | Founders & Licensing Action Plan

Priority 1: Legal Structure & Ownership

Owners: Jay West and Tony
Potential Third Owner: Daniel Soliz
Business: Texas Public Insurance Adjusting Agency

Step 1: Establish the LLC
Confirm whether FlatRatePA LLC already exists with the Texas Secretary of State.
Official resources: Texas Secretary of State and IRS EIN Application.

Step 2: Decide Ownership
Recommendation: Option A initially. Jay and Tony retain ownership while Daniel serves as the responsible licensed officer under a separate employment or service agreement. Daniel's officer appointment and ownership arrangement must satisfy Texas agency licensing requirements.

Step 3: Draft the Operating Agreement
Ownership, Contributions, Responsibilities, Decision Rights, Profit Distribution, Compensation, Vesting, Exit Terms, Dispute Resolution, Daniel's Position.
Suggested management model: Manager-managed LLC. Jay oversees technology and operations. Tony oversees sales and partnerships. Daniel has documented authority over licensed adjusting and regulatory compliance.

Priority 2: Agency Licensing
Daniel already obtained his individual Texas public adjuster license. The business needs its own agency license.
Step 1: Collect Daniel's Documentation, verify via Texas Department of Insurance.
Step 2: Obtain Agency Bond - FlatRatePA LLC needs its own $10,000 public adjusting agency surety bond, separate from Daniel's individual bond, identifying the correct legal entity. Owner: Jay, working with Daniel.
Step 3: Submit Agency Application per Texas Public Insurance Adjuster Licensing instructions.
Completion criterion: TDI issues an active public insurance adjusting agency license to FlatRatePA LLC.

Priority 3: Contracts & Compensation
A. Homeowner Agreement - Daniel should approve the contract's scope and fee provisions, with Texas insurance counsel confirming compliance before use.
B. Daniel's Compensation Agreement - separate Ownership (equity), Compensation (payment for licensed services), and Authority (independent responsibility for regulated claim decisions). Do not automatically promise Daniel $100 per claim until the arrangement has been reviewed.
C. Roofer Partnership Agreement - Tony should own these relationships.
