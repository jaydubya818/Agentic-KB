MyEve is the persistent digital worker—the intelligence, identity, memory, and owner of the work. Sofie remembers the user, projects, decisions, preferences, prior work, and lessons learned through total-recall memory, a local knowledge graph, file-based memory, and qualified continuous learning. Through Role Packs such as Software Engineer or Founder, capability packs such as JStack, and modes such as /potato-mode, Sofie can understand objectives, gather context from RAG, MCPs, GitHub, Jira, Linear, files, and connected apps, then plan and execute work proactively. Work persists beyond any chat, model, computer, or individual execution session, and Sofie continuously improves from feedback while keeping authority, credentials, and organizational boundaries deterministic and separate from learned behavior.

MyFactory is the software-production engine MyEve can use when work requires substantial engineering execution. Sofie can develop directly herself, use a specialized coding executor, or send a larger objective to MyFactory, which can plan the objective, decompose it into WorkOrders and tasks, route work to appropriate executors, implement changes, run verification, and return candidates, artifacts, and evidence. MyEve remains responsible for the overall Work and the human relationship: it tracks acceptance criteria, reconciles results, monitors CI and review feedback, recovers from failures, requests approval when necessary, and produces an evidence-backed Proof of Work when the result is genuinely ready for human review. In short: MyFactory produces software; MyEve owns the responsibility.

Relay is the communication and trust fabric that lets MyEve safely work with other digital workers and specialist agents. Sofie can discover and communicate with agents such as Atlas, engineering standards, security, QA, platform, or another person’s MyEve to exchange approved knowledge, request bounded research or work, obtain reviews, and share artifacts—all with explicit identity, permissions, provenance, revocation, and privacy boundaries. Together, the architecture becomes: MyEve remembers, reasons, coordinates, learns, and owns the work; MyFactory plans and produces complex software; Relay connects MyEve to trusted agents and their knowledge/capabilities. The result is a persistent digital worker that can be given responsibility, keep working when the human leaves, collaborate and recover autonomously, return only when judgment is genuinely needed, and become progressively more useful through qualified experience.

Product Vision
MyEve should become a persistent personal digital worker platform.
The first opinionated product built on that platform is:
MyEve Engineering
A persistent AI Software Engineer that understands your work, remembers what matters, uses tools and software factories to execute it, collaborates with other Agents, continuously learns from experience, and brings work back with evidence when human attention is required.
The MVP must prove this experience without attempting to build the entire long-term platform.
Core positioning
Delegate work, not just prompts.
For Engineering:
Give Sofie engineering work. She understands it, works it, collaborates when necessary, verifies the result, and returns when the work is ready for you.

Architectural Principles
Preserve these invariants:
Identity persists. Memory accumulates. Roles specialize. Modes change behavior. Skills add capability. Tools execute. Relay connects. MyFactory produces. Policy authorizes. Evidence proves.
And:
Work is durable. Compute is replaceable.
Agents reason. Deterministic systems authorize.
Agents propose completion. Evidence determines readiness.
Executors create candidates. Trusted systems publish them.
Experience proposes learning. Evidence qualifies learning.
Authority is never learned.
