What I’d build next: AVF Autonomous Company Builder

This should become a permanent AVF capability, not something custom-built for Fairhome.

AVF should be able to take any approved venture and generate a complete Company Launch WorkOrder.

That WorkOrder becomes the execution contract for the entire business-building process.

For example:

Venture: Fairhome
Investment status: Approved
Business model: Full-service property tax appeals
Launch market: Selected through research
Owner: You
Execution mode: Autonomous within approved authority

AVF would then coordinate parallel workstreams.

Business formation: Name clearance, entity structure, formation paperwork, EIN preparation, registered-agent setup, insurance requirements, banking onboarding, and accounting.

Product engineering: MyEve, Relay, and Software Factory develop the website, CRM, property analysis engine, homeowner dashboard, professional review console, billing integrations, and AI operations.

Revenue infrastructure: Payment processing, customer agreements, fee calculations, invoices, refunds, collections, and financial reporting.

Growth: Search engine optimization, content, conversion optimization, customer acquisition, referral programs, email campaigns, and analytics.

Operations: Customer onboarding, case processing, support, quality assurance, monitoring, professional escalation, and performance reporting.

These workstreams should execute concurrently wherever dependencies permit.
