PART 10 — QUESTIONS TO ASK AUSTIN PENNA

48. WHAT QUESTIONS SHOULD YOU ASK AUSTIN?

Likelihood: CERTAIN | Reserve 5–10 minutes

Your questions should demonstrate Workday expertise, product ownership, strategic thinking, technical depth, and enthusiasm for building an AI-native People Technology function.

The goal is to make Austin see you as someone who can help execute his vision, independently own the People Systems portfolio, and deliver immediate business value.

⸻

QUESTION 1 — HIS VISION ⭐ TOP PRIORITY

AI-Native HR · Strategic Alignment · Innovation

“I read your post about building an AI-native HR function from a blank canvas, and that really resonated with me.

If you could redesign one employee workflow from the ground up, which would you start with and why?”

OPTIONAL FOLLOW-UP:

“Do you envision AI primarily improving existing Workday workflows, or do you see an opportunity to fundamentally rethink how employees and managers interact with People Technology?”

WHY THIS MATTERS:

Demonstrates that you understand his vision and are thinking beyond traditional HRIS administration.

⸻

QUESTION 2 — WORKDAY MATURITY ⭐ TOP PRIORITY

Workday Architecture · Configuration · Platform Ownership

“Where is Nscale today with its Workday implementation and configuration?

Is the immediate priority building foundational capabilities, stabilizing existing processes, or expanding Workday functionality?”

OPTIONAL FOLLOW-UP:

“Which Workday modules are currently implemented, and which capabilities do you anticipate introducing over the next 6–12 months?”

WHY THIS MATTERS:

Helps you understand the actual state of the Workday environment and where your expertise would provide the most immediate value.

⸻

QUESTION 3 — FIRST 90 DAYS ⭐ TOP PRIORITY

Expectations · Immediate Priorities · Business Outcomes

“If I joined your team, what would be the three most important things you’d want me to accomplish in my first 90 days?”

OPTIONAL FOLLOW-UP:

“Are there any immediate challenges involving Workday configuration, integrations, data quality, or employee workflows that you would want me to prioritize?”

WHY THIS MATTERS:

Positions you as someone focused on execution, accountability, and measurable outcomes.

⸻

QUESTION 4 — PEOPLE SYSTEMS ARCHITECTURE

Workday · Greenhouse · Integrations · Employee Lifecycle

“How are you envisioning the broader People Systems architecture as Nscale continues scaling?

Will Workday remain the central system of record, with platforms like Greenhouse supporting specialized capabilities, or are you considering a different architectural approach?”

OPTIONAL FOLLOW-UP:

“Are there particular integration challenges between Workday, Greenhouse, payroll, identity management, and other employee systems that you’re already looking to address?”

WHY THIS MATTERS:

Demonstrates enterprise architecture experience and an understanding of the complete employee lifecycle.

⸻

Configuration · Business Processes · Security · Reporting
