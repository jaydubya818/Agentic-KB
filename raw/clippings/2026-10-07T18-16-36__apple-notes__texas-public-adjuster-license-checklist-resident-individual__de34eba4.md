Texas Public Adjuster License Checklist (resident individual)
0. Eligibility
You are 18 or older.
You are a U.S. citizen, or you comply with federal rules on working and doing business in the U.S.
You are a Texas resident.
You have no felony conviction in the last 10 years, unless fully pardoned. A felony 11 or more years old can still be approved at the Commissioner's discretion.
You are of good moral character (TDI judges this).
You have enough experience or training to assess property values and physical loss or damage.
You understand property insurance contracts.
You are not a contractor on the same property. A contractor can't act as, or advertise as, a public adjuster on property they do or may repair.
You use your legal name. TDI does not accept a DBA.
1. Pass the exam first
Download the candidate handbook and content outline from Pearson VUE (Texas insurance page).
Study the topics: insurance theory, contracts, claims ethics, Ins. Code Ch. 541, Ch. 542 (Subchapters A and B), Ch. 547, the DTPA, unauthorized practice of law, and adjuster duties.
Register, pay, and schedule. Pearson VUE's number is in the candidate handbook.
If you need ESL or Spanish, file the ESL request form.
Veterans can get the exam fee reimbursed through the VA.
Apply within 1 year of passing, or you must retake the exam.
Don't apply before you pass. If you do, you'll have to reapply and pay the $50 fee again.
2. Fingerprints
Start on TDI's online application and fingerprint portal. TDI will email you a service code.
Book IdentoGO online (choose "Digital Fingerprinting") or call the number on IdentoGO's site.
Bring a photo ID and payment. They take cards, business checks, and money orders. They don't take cash or personal checks.
Keep the IdentoGO receipt. TDI won't accept an enrollment form or payment confirmation in its place.
You can skip fingerprints if you already live in Texas and hold an active TDI license or registration with prints on file.
Hard-card ink prints are only for when electronic prints aren't available. Mail them to IdentoGO, not TDI.
3. Bond
Get a surety bond of $10,000 or more, with you as the sole principal.
Complete Form FIN509 (Public Insurance Adjuster Bond).
Keep a copy of the original bond form. You'll upload both.
4. Client contract
Either use TDI's Form FIN535 (mark it on the application) or upload your own contract that meets TAC §19.708.
The contract must give the client a 72-hour right to rescind.
It must show "WE REPRESENT THE INSURED ONLY." in 12-point boldface.
5. Apply on Sircon
Apply online on Sircon and pay the $50 application fee.
Upload the bond (FIN509 and the original), the contract choice, and the IdentoGO receipt.
The statute says the application must be notarized, and you must authorize TDI to see your fiduciary financial records. Follow whatever the Sircon flow asks for.
After approval, print your license from Sircon.
If you're applying as an agency or nonresident
Agency:
You need a Texas designated responsible licensed producer (DRLP): an officer or active partner who holds a Texas license.
You need info on all officers, directors, partners, and controlling parties.
You need fingerprints for any who are unlicensed.
You need Texas Secretary of State registration.
You need a $10,000 bond in the exact agency name.
You need a contract choice.
Limited partnerships and LLPs need their partnership agreement.
