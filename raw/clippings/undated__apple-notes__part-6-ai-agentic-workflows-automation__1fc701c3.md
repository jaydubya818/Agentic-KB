TECHNICAL FOLLOW-UP
Austin: “What’s the difference between traditional automation and an AI agent?”
30-SECOND RESPONSE
“Traditional automation follows predefined rules and workflows. It’s ideal for predictable processes like approvals, notifications, and data synchronization.
An AI agent uses a model to interpret context, reason through a task, select appropriate tools, and execute multiple steps toward a defined goal.
For example, traditional automation can trigger an onboarding checklist when an employee is hired.
An AI agent can understand an employee’s question, retrieve relevant policies, identify missing onboarding tasks, and guide the employee through the process.
I use deterministic automation for structured transactions and AI agents where interpretation, knowledge retrieval, or adaptive decision-making adds value.”
