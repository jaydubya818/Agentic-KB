22. BUILDING PEOPLE TECHNOLOGY FROM A BLANK CANVAS
HOW WOULD YOU APPROACH BUILDING NSCALE’S PEOPLE TECHNOLOGY FUNCTION FROM A BLANK WORKDAY CANVAS?
Likelihood: EXTREMELY HIGH | 60–90 seconds
KEYWORDS
People Technology Strategy | Core HCM | Greenhouse | Employee Lifecycle | Integrations | Governance | AI-Native Workflows
TALKING SCRIPT
“I’d approach it as an opportunity to build an AI-native People Technology function, rather than simply implementing another HR system.
First, I’d partner with People Operations, Talent Acquisition, Finance, IT, and Security to understand Nscale’s growth plans, current processes, and biggest operational challenges.
Second, I’d establish the Workday Core HCM foundation, including supervisory organizations, staffing models, job architecture, business processes, security, and reporting.
Third, I’d map the end-to-end employee lifecycle, starting with recruiting, hiring, onboarding, employee changes, and offboarding, identifying where Workday connects with Greenhouse and downstream systems.
Fourth, I’d establish data governance, integration standards, security controls, and release management so the platform remains reliable as the company scales.
Finally, I’d identify opportunities for AI agents, copilots, and intelligent workflow automation, prioritizing high-volume, repetitive processes where AI can deliver measurable value.
At Workday, I led AI platforms supporting 300-plus engineering teams, reducing manual effort by 35% and AI execution costs by 45%.
My goal would be to build a secure, scalable People Technology foundation while introducing practical AI capabilities that improve the employee experience from day one.”
KEY MESSAGE
Understand Business Needs → Establish Core HCM → Connect People Systems → Governance → AI Automation → Scale

LIKELY FOLLOW-UP
Austin: “If you could only prioritize three things in your first 90 days, what would they be?”
30–45 SECOND RESPONSE
“I’d prioritize three things.
First, understand the current environment. I’d assess Workday configuration, existing integrations, data quality, security, and the most important employee workflows.
Second, stabilize and establish the foundation. I’d address critical gaps in Core HCM, Hire and Change Job business processes, Greenhouse integrations, and reporting.
Third, deliver one measurable improvement. I’d select a high-friction workflow, such as candidate-to-employee onboarding, and improve it through configuration, integration, or automation.
By day 90, I’d want a clear People Systems roadmap, reliable core workflows, and at least one measurable operational improvement.”
