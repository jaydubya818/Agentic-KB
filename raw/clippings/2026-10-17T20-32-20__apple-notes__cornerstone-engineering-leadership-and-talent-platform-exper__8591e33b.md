2. THE ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

Talent processes are highly interconnected.

Talent Systems and Talent Management depends on:

Positions
Candidate data
Onboarding
Learning
Compensation
Reporting
Permissions
Employee data
Org structures
Integrations
Downstream workflows

A feature could not simply work within one application.

It had to:

Support the complete business process
Integrate with adjacent products
Preserve data integrity
Respect permissions
Support different customer configurations
Alway be reliable and supportable at enterprise scale

That shaped how I led Engineering.

I focused not only on whether a team could build a feature, but whether the feature was:

Integrated. Validated. Secure. Configurable. Observable. Supportable. Reliable. Ready for enterprise customers.

Strong Line

“Local component success does not guarantee system success. A product is only reliable when the full customer workflow works.”
