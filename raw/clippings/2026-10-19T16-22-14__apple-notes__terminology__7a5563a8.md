How to troubleshoot a Hire BP

1. Find the BP Event: Locate the Hire transaction.

2. Open Details and Process → Process: Review step statuses and responsible users.

3. Identify the issue: Determine whether a step is pending, skipped, delayed, or incorrectly routed.

4. Review BP Definition: Check approvals, condition rules, and routing.

5. Validate Security: Confirm role assignments and BP permissions.

6. Test in Sandbox: Verify the corrected workflow.

7. Validate Integrations: Confirm downstream employee data is accurate.
