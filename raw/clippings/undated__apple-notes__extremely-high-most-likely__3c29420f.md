NSCALE — PRODUCT MANAGER, PEOPLE SYSTEMS

Job Requirements Explained — Quick Cheat Sheet

1. Own the People Systems Roadmap

What it means: Decide what gets built, improved, or integrated across Workday, Greenhouse, and other HR systems.

Key responsibilities:

Define the product strategy and roadmap.
Prioritize features based on business value, urgency, and impact.
Manage the product backlog and delivery timelines.
Balance new functionality with technical maintenance.

Interview takeaway: Demonstrate product ownership, prioritization, and strategic decision-making.

⸻

2. Partner With People Teams to Improve Workflows

What it means: Work directly with HR, Recruiting, Payroll, Finance, and IT to improve employee processes.

Key responsibilities:

Understand business requirements and pain points.
Design end-to-end employee lifecycle workflows.
Improve hiring, onboarding, transfers, promotions, and offboarding.
Measure success using KPIs, automation rates, and employee experience.

Interview takeaway: Demonstrate stakeholder collaboration, business process improvement, and measurable results.

⸻

3. Configure Workday and Build Integrations

What it means: Be technically hands-on with Workday configuration, integrations, reporting, and automation.

Key responsibilities:

Core HCM: Supervisory organizations, staffing models, job profiles.
Business Processes: Configure approvals, routing, conditions, and notifications.
Security: Configure security groups and domain/BP permissions.
Reporting: Create custom reports, calculated fields, and dashboards.
Integrations: Connect Workday with Greenhouse using EIB, APIs, RaaS, and Studio.
AI/Agents: Automate HR workflows using AI and agentic capabilities.

Interview takeaway: This is likely the most technically important requirement. Austin will want evidence of your hands-on Workday knowledge.

⸻

4. Establish Data Governance and Security

What it means: Ensure employee data is accurate, protected, compliant, and auditable.

Key responsibilities:

Implement role-based access control (RBAC) and least privilege.
Protect sensitive employee information.
Maintain data quality, integrity
