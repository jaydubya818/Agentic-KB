Top 10 Workday Questions 

2. HOW HANDS-ON ARE YOU WITH WORKDAY? WHAT HAVE YOU PERSONALLY CONFIGURED?

Likelihood: EXTREMELY HIGH | 60–90 seconds

TALKING SCRIPT

“My Workday experience spans Core HCM workflows, business processes, security validation, integrations, automation, and release management.

At Workday, I led engineering teams responsible for validating Core HCM business processes, security-group permissions, EIB data loads, Studio integrations, and tenant releases.

We focused on ensuring employee lifecycle workflows operated correctly across Workday and connected enterprise systems.

At Cornerstone, I owned integration architecture connecting Workday with Greenhouse, Oracle, SAP SuccessFactors, ADP, ServiceNow, and Okta.

That included candidate-to-employee handoffs, hiring approvals, employee data synchronization, payroll integrations, and identity provisioning.

My strongest hands-on experience is in integration architecture, workflow validation, security testing, automation, and platform reliability.

I want to distinguish that from being the sole administrator of a customer Workday tenant.

My background is broader across engineering, integrations, product ownership, and operational reliability, rather than exclusively administering Core HCM configuration.

What I bring is a strong understanding of how Workday’s configuration, business processes, security, and integrations work together, along with the ability to troubleshoot issues and own the end-to-end outcome.”

LIKELY FOLLOW-UP

Austin: “Could I give you access to our Workday tenant tomorrow and have you independently configure a business process?”

YOUR ANSWER:

“I’d be comfortable assessing the existing configuration, understanding the business requirements, identifying the necessary changes, and validating the workflow.

For configuration changes I haven’t personally executed before, I’d review the tenant-specific setup and supported procedures before making production changes.

I wouldn’t represent validation or engineering oversight as direct administration experience, but I’m comfortable taking technical ownership and working through unfamiliar configuration areas.”

KEY MESSAGE: Strong Workday integration and engineering expertise, with transparency about direct tenant administration.
