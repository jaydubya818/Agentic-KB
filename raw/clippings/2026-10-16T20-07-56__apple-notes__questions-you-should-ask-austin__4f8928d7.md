QUESTIONS YOU SHOULD ASK AUSTIN

These questions will help you understand the role while demonstrating your technical and product-management experience.

Is the Workday tenant live or mid-implementation? What's in scope beyond Core HCM, like payroll, benefits, comp, or an EOR for global hires?
What's in the stack today besides Greenhouse and MetaView: payroll provider, IdP, benefits, and an SI or partner?
How will the two PM hires split the portfolio?
What would make you say this hire was a home run at six months?
What does "AI-native HR" look like in practice for you? Which workflow would you rebuild first?
Are you building on Workday Extend, Workday's agents, or your own platform with Corporate Engineering?




QUESTION 1 — HIS VISION

“You helped define this role. What are the three most important outcomes you’d want me to deliver in the first six months?”

QUESTION 2 — CURRENT WORKDAY ENVIRONMENT

“Which Workday modules are currently live, and how mature are your business processes, security configuration, reporting, and integrations?”

QUESTION 3 — HANDS-ON EXPECTATIONS

“How much of this position is focused on hands-on Workday configuration versus product ownership, architecture, integrations, and automation?”

QUESTION 4 — TEAM STRUCTURE

“How is the People Technology organization structured today, and what HRIS, integration, or engineering resources would support this role?”

QUESTION 5 — TWO OPEN POSITIONS

“Shafaq mentioned you’re hiring two people for this position, potentially with different experience levels. How do you envision dividing ownership and responsibilities between them?”

QUESTION 6 — CURRENT CHALLENGES

“What are the biggest challenges you’re experiencing today with Workday, Greenhouse, and the broader employee lifecycle?”

QUESTION 7 — AUTOMATION AND AI

“Where do you see the strongest opportunities to introduce automation or AI into People Operations over the next year?”

QUESTION 8 — SCALING

“Given Nscale’s rapid growth, what parts of the People Systems architecture concern you most as the organization scales?”

QUESTION 9 — SUCCESS METRICS

“How will you measure success for this position after 90 days and after the first year?”

QUESTION 10 — CLOSING QUESTION

“Based on our conversation, is there anything about my background or experience that you’d like me to clarify to help you assess my fit for the role?”
