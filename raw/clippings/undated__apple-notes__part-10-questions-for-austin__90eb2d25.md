HOW MUCH OF THIS ROLE WILL INVOLVE HANDS-ON WORKDAY CONFIGURATION VERSUS PRODUCT STRATEGY, INTEGRATIONS, AND AI DEVELOPMENT?

Priority: EXTREMELY HIGH

KEYWORDS

Hands-On Configuration | Product Ownership | Integrations | AI Development | Prioritization | Execution

TALKING SCRIPT

“One of the things that attracted me to this opportunity is the combination of hands-on Workday configuration, product ownership, integrations, and AI development.

I’m curious how you envision that balance in practice.

During the first six months, where do you expect this person to spend most of their time?

And as the People Technology function grows, how do you see those responsibilities evolving?”

WHY THIS QUESTION MATTERS

Austin has emphasized the need for someone who can independently configure Workday.

This question helps clarify whether the role is primarily:

Hands-on Workday administration and configuration
People Systems product management
Integration architecture and engineering
AI and agentic workflow development

It also helps you understand whether the role will evolve as Nscale builds its People Technology organization.

POTENTIAL FOLLOW-UP

If Austin emphasizes hands-on Workday configuration:

“That’s helpful. Which areas are the most immediate priorities — Core HCM business processes, security, reporting, integrations, or ongoing tenant administration?”

KEY MESSAGE

Clarify Responsibilities → Understand Immediate Needs → Align Expectations
