The two architectural priorities Shibu gave you should be impossible to miss

I’d actually put a small callout very early in the printed book:

Two immediate Meta Factory architecture problems

1. Economics: Give the factory architectural levers to dynamically optimize quality, cost, latency and reliability rather than maximizing model capability or minimizing token price in isolation.

2. Secure execution: Create a sandboxed remote-execution substrate that allows agents to operate at Adobe scale without inheriting uncontrolled filesystem, network, credential, repository or production authority.

Those shouldn’t just appear in Chapters 19 and 25. They should echo throughout the book.
