RoofClaim Recovery

Business proposition:
A public adjusting + AI-powered claim recovery company focused initially on residential roofing claims. We help homeowners and roofing companies recover more money from insurance carriers, faster and with less administrative work.

Problem:
Roof claims are messy. Homeowners don’t understand policies, roofers aren’t insurance-claim experts, documentation is inconsistent, and carriers frequently deny, delay, or underpay claims.

Solution:
RoofClaim Recovery handles the claim lifecycle:

Inspect → Document → Analyze Policy → Build Scope → Submit/Supplement → Negotiate → Recover

Over time, RecoveryOS + Recovery Intelligence automate much of that process and learn which evidence, strategies, and workflows produce better outcomes.

Who pays:
Two primary customer channels:

Homeowners: RoofClaim Recovery represents them and earns a percentage of recovered claim proceeds where permitted.
Roofing companies: Become referral/strategic partners because you help their customers get legitimate claims resolved and help roofers reduce claim-administration headaches.

Core value proposition:

You fix the roof. We fight for the claim.

For homeowners:

Denied, delayed, or underpaid roof claim? We help you recover what your policy actually covers.

Marketing strategy:
I would prioritize roofing partnerships first, then homeowner demand generation. One productive roofer can continuously send claims; acquiring homeowners individually is much more expensive.

Start with 20–50 local roofing companies → free claim reviews → demonstrate recoveries → testimonials/case studies → referral engine → geographic expansion.

For homeowners, dominate high-intent searches around “roof claim denied,” “insurance underpaid roof claim,” “roof insurance supplement,” and storm-specific searches. Use Google Search/Local, SEO, educational content, direct mail after storms, and retargeting.

The bigger business:
The public adjusting firm generates revenue + proprietary claims data. RecoveryOS creates operational scale. Recovery Intelligence becomes the data moat.

Service → Software → Intelligence.

That is what makes RoofClaim Recovery potentially much larger than a traditional public adjusting firm.
